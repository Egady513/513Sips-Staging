Wedding bar questions

What couples usually ask.

Does 513Sips provide the alcohol?

513Sips is a dry-hire bartending service, so you purchase the alcohol. We can help estimate quantities and can coordinate sourcing when that service is included in your package or quote.

How many bartenders does my wedding need?

It depends on guest count, service style, venue layout and your drink menu. We confirm the staffing plan during quoting rather than using one rule for every wedding.

Can you create signature cocktails?

Yes. Signature cocktail service can include recipe planning, house-made syrups, garnishes and a custom menu card depending on your package.

Will you coordinate with our venue?

Yes. We can confirm access, setup needs, service timing and proof of insurance with your venue so the bar plan matches its requirements.

Are you insured and certified?

513Sips carries event liability coverage and lists ASK, TIPS and food-safety certifications on the main website. Proof of insurance is available for venue coordination.

What areas do you serve?

513Sips serves Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky. Standard packages include travel within 30 miles. Ask about your venue when you inquire.
