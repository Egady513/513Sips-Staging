How Dry-Hire Works

We don't profit on alcohol. That is the whole model.

We bring the bar, the bartenders and the experience, and the alcohol is bought at cost. It does not matter who buys it, only that it is bought at retail, which is where the savings live. A bottle that runs $22 at Costco can carry a $60+ open-bar charge anywhere else. Don't want to make the run? We build the list, do the shopping, hand over every receipt and return whatever goes unopened.
